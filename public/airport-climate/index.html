How this relates to Scopes 1, 2 and 3

Scopes describe emissions relative to an organisation: direct emissions (1), purchased energy (2), and other indirect value-chain emissions (3). An airline's fuel burn is its Scope 1; aircraft emissions can be an airport operator's Scope 3. Do not add those overlapping inventories. A national carbon budget is a different accounting and legal framework, not a corporate scope.

Airport operational net zero often covers the operator's Scopes 1 and 2. Check wider promises separately: coverage does not itself establish a restriction on expansion.
